Generational Coastal Palms

Daily Morning Plucking from Mineral-Rich Soils

Exceptional drinks begin with fertile soils, sea breezes, and healthy palms. Our green tender coconuts and fruits are harvested from mineral-rich coastal groves where tall-variety palms thrive under natural tropical rainfall.

Harvested in early morning hours, green coconuts reach our stall within hours. We check each nut for full water volume and tender jelly-like malai, cutting them open live to order with zero concentrates, zero additives, and zero compromise.

The Fresh Journey

From Palm to Glass: 6-Step Journey

A time-sensitive, hygiene-focused workflow from orchard dawn plucking to live stall decapping.

01

1. Coastal Grove Harvest

Harvested at dawn in coastal palm orchards where mineral-rich soils and sea breezes nurture sweet electrolyte-packed young nuts.

02

2. Stall Grading & Inspection

Coconuts are hand-tested for weight and water slosh to ensure heavy sweet water and delicate spoon-tender coconut meat.

03

3. Live Decapping & Pressing

Green coconuts are sliced open live with clean machetes, and fresh sugarcane or fruit is pressed through stainless steel rollers.

04

4. 100% Zero-Sugar Purity

Zero white sugar, zero water dilution, and zero artificial preservatives. Only the wholesome sweetness of fresh tropical fruit.

05

5. Eco Husk & Glass Serving

Sip in-shell through a biodegradable paper straw, or take home freshly bottled raw coconut water in sterilized glass bottles.

06

6. Stall Pickup & Event Catering

Walk up to our neighborhood counter for daily refreshment, or book our live stall counter for weddings, sports meets, and galas.

Stall Philosophy

Freshness Integrity & Strict Stall Hygiene

We believe great juicing requires direct grove relationships, strict sanitation, and complete ingredient honesty.

Farm-Direct Daily Harvest

Long-term partnerships with smallholder coconut farmers ensure daily deliveries of freshly harvested, unblemished green coconuts.

Zero-Waste Coconut Stewardship

Discarded green coconut shells and husks are gathered daily and sent to agricultural composters and natural coir manufacturers.

Strict Cold Serving & Filtered Ice

Tender coconuts are naturally chilled to 4°C; our juice counters exclusively use purified RO water and sanitized stainless steel.

Zero-Waste Ecology

100% Circular Coconut Husk Recycling

Not a single green husk enters a landfill. Every spent shell from our counter is collected daily, sun-dried, and converted into natural coir garden mulch and organic grove compost to nourish the next generation of palms.
