Coastal Grove Sourcing & Terroir

Harvested at Peak Maturity. Pressed the Same Day.

Great coconut products begin with the tree and the soil. Our coconuts are harvested from mineral-rich coastal groves where tall-variety palms thrive under sea breezes, tropical rainfall, and regenerative intercropped soils.

By working in direct partnership with multi-generational smallholder farming families, we coordinate harvests to ensure mature 12-month coconuts are de-husked, cracked, and cold-processed within 6 hours—guaranteeing peak nutrient density and zero off-flavors.

The Production Journey

From Coconut to Product: 6-Step Process

A rigorous, time-sensitive workflow that respects the natural chemistry of fresh coconut kernel.

01

1. Coastal Grove Sourcing

Harvested in 45-day cycles from coastal groves where sea-breeze microclimates and volcanic soils nurture nutrient-dense tall palms.

02

2. Selection of Mature Nuts

Nuts are hand-inspected post-husking. Only fully matured 12-month coconuts with thick snowy-white meat are selected for our oils, chips, and desiccated cuts.

03

3. Processing & Cold Extraction

Fresh white kernel is washed in purified water and immediately cold-pressed or spun in chilled centrifuges without heat or chemical solvents.

04

4. Product Preparation

Cuts are grated into fine/medium shreds, sliced into ribbons for rotary oven toasting, or stone-ground for 18 hours into velvet cream.

05

5. Protective Packaging

Oils and tender coconut water are filled into inert flint or amber glass, while dry foods are sealed in high-barrier recyclable craft pouches.

06

6. Traceable Delivery

Every container bears a batch identifier, dispatching from climate-controlled storage in plastic-free molded pulp shippers.

Sourcing Philosophy

Responsible Principles & Production Integrity

We believe ethical sourcing requires verifiable actions, long-term grower partnerships, and complete production honesty.

Responsible Sourcing Principles

Long-term purchase commitments with smallholder growers protect against agricultural price volatility and support agroforestry biodiversity.

Zero-Waste Coconut Utilization

Fibrous coir husks enrich grove soil as moisture-retaining organic mulch, while shells provide clean thermal energy for gentle dehydration.

Strict Cold Extraction Limits

Centrifuge oil temperature never exceeds 26°C; mechanical expeller cold pressing remains strictly under 42°C (107°F).
