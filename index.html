The COCOAURA Standard

Why Choose COCOAURA

We focus on ingredient honesty, clean mechanical processing, and protective packaging—without industrial refining or exaggerated claims.

Carefully Selected Coconuts

Each harvest is hand-graded at optimal maturity—12 months for dense oil-rich kernel and 7 months for crisp coconut water.

Thoughtful Sourcing

From Coastal Grove to Glass Jar

Harvested at Peak Maturity. Pressed the Same Day.

Great coconut oil begins long before extraction. In our partner coastal groves, tall-variety palms grow in mineral-rich volcanic and sandy loam soils fed by tropical rainfall.

Once harvested, mature nuts are husked, split by hand, and inspected for kernel thickness. By cold-pressing and spinning the fresh coconut milk within hours of cracking the shell, we avoid the stale, smoky off-notes common in copra-derived oils.

Zero Copra Drying Pressed exclusively from fresh, snowy-white wet coconut kernel.
