Stall Services, Hygiene, Event Bookings & Freshness

Every single green coconut is harvested daily from coastal palm groves and brought straight to our stall. Juices and tender coconuts are cut and cold-pressed live in front of you when you order—never pre-bottled days in advance, with zero added sugar, artificial colors, or chemical preservatives.

Yes! Our Live Tender Coconut & Fresh Juice Stall setup is one of our most popular services for weddings, birthday parties, sports meets, and corporate wellness events. We bring our rustic wooden counter, skilled coconut cutters, cold presses, and hundreds of fresh green coconuts branded with custom event stamps or flags. Inquire via our form above for custom packages.
We adhere to strict food safety protocols: stainless steel tools sanitized throughout the day, filtered ice only, compostable paper straws, wooden spoons, and food-grade glass takeaway bottles. The empty green coconut husks are collected daily for organic composting and coir recycling.
Because our juices and bottled coconut water are 100% raw and unpasteurized with zero preservatives, keep them refrigerated below 4°C. Raw tender coconut water is best consumed within 48 hours, and cold-pressed fruit juices within 24–36 hours for peak freshness and nutritional vitality.
