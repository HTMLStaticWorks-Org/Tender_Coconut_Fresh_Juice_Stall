Bulk Orders, Packaging, Shipping & Products

Pure, unfractionated coconut oil has a natural melting point of approximately 24°C (76°F). In cooler rooms or winter months, it solidifies into a creamy snow-white butter; in warmer kitchens, it melts into a crystal-clear liquid. Transitioning between states is a natural hallmark of unrefined coconut oil and does not affect quality or shelf life.
Our Artisanal Raw Virgin Coconut Oil is extracted from fresh wet coconut milk using chilled high-speed centrifuges, yielding an ultra-light mouthfeel and delicate fresh coconut aroma. Our Estate Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil is expeller-pressed below 42°C from gently dried mature coconut kernel, offering a slightly warmer, nutty culinary note.
For retail-ready case packs (glass jars, bottles, and pouches), our sample wholesale MOQ starts at 4 master cases per SKU. For foodservice and manufacturing formats (5L/20L oil pails, 200L drums, and 10kg/25kg desiccated coconut kraft sacks), MOQs start at 100 kg or 1 pallet.
